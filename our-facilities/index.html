We have facilities in Germany, the UK and Poland. Our state of the art pilot plant facilities are located in Lübeck, Germany and Cheshire, UK and from these sites we develop bespoke stabiliser system solutions for food manufacturers - looking to create products which will help them succeed in the market place. At both of these sites we have a dedicated technical development suite where we are able to fully replicate customer production environments.

We also have a dedicated, purpose built production and Warehouse site in Reinfeld, North Germany, from where we are able to supply the stabiliser systems our customers use in their final products.

Our subsidiary in Sady near Poznań, Poland serves our customers in the Polish market. Finished products are shipped to our customers from a spacious humidity and temperature controlled warehouse.

Our Facilities are fully certified to meet the needs of food manufacturers. Full details of our Quality Standards and Certificates can be found here.
