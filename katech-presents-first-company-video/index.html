KaTech presents first company video

People, sites and expertise at KaTech are shown

Lübeck/ Chester, 20th July 2016: KaTech Katharina Hahn + Partner have come out with a short company film which is available from today on on their website showing their approach, people, sites and expertise. The video can be watched on their website at www.katech.info.

"Visualising how people are working in our business, and giving insight into the core of our business model and approach is something we were excited to do. We are a young and modern business that has built on traditional values that our markets appreciate. This is also demonstrated in how we are using modern marketing and communication tools to show the duality of our traditional yet modern approach." says Patrick Schwarz Managing Director Germany.

KaTech wanted to communicate more widely about the company after their very successful first 5 years after having started the business in 2011.

KaTech Katharina Hahn + Partner is a food technology company whose business is the design, manufacture and delivery of tailor-made food stabilising solutions to the international food industry. The European based business, bought by Katharina Hahn in 2012, specialises in Savoury foods, all Dairy products and Bakery.
