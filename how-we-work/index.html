How we work

Working with you, to deliver on your objectives.

KaTech develops bespoke food technology solutions designed to meet your exact needs and deliver your objectives; bring products to market, help you solve any problems and to optimise product costs. Working.

In practice

Sounds good. What does that mean on a Tuesday?

Four steps, and the same people through all of them.

  1. 01

    Understand the brief

    Your end product goals, your process capabilities and the raw materials you already buy. Nothing is designed against a line that cannot run it.

  2. 02

    Formulate and trial

    Development in our pilot plants in Lübeck and Cheshire, on equipment that behaves like production equipment. Iterations happen in days, not quarters.

  3. 03

    Prove it on your line

    Our technologists come to your factory for the scale-up trial. The people who designed the system are the people standing next to it.

  4. 04

    Supply and support

    Blending and supply from our allergen-controlled production site, with the technical contact you already know.

Sounds good...but what does that mean in practice?

We are a business that puts its focus on our customers success. When our customers are successful, we share in that success. To deliver bespoke solutions we take account of your end product goals, your process capabilities and raw materials. Replicating your process and recipe in controlled pilot conditions is the starting point for our technical teams. Maintaining a strong awareness of raw material markets we develop product solutions that not only meet the product brief, but are also based on secure and commercially viable raw material sources.

Our Technical Sales and Technical staff are all 'hands-on' with many years experience in real world production environments. They support the development process from idea conception to factory trials whether this is needed at pilot level or on site at your premises. This experience allows them to speak with authority, giving confidence at each step whilst always playing a supporting role to you in your business.

We have a wealth of knowledge and expertise in producing a variety of products for the food industry. Our team at KaTech work with food technologists and process engineers at leading food manufacturers and partnership is key to our approach - we work side by side with your team to develop winning solutions.
